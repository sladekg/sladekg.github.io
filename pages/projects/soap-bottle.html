Design for Sustainability · Product concept

A bottle that leaves less behind.

A regenerative hand-soap package made from recycled hotel soaps, designed to reduce waste one bottle at a time.

Product designSustainabilityResearch

The idea

Use the package, not just what’s inside.

Inspired by New Zealand environmentalist culture, I designed a product around an everyday waste problem: what happens to a bottle after the soap is gone?

Made using recycled hotel soaps, the bottle includes a one-time-purchase squirt top. Once the liquid is removed, the outside of the bottle can be used up too, reducing waste throughout the product's life.

The concept was developed through student iteration toward theoretical publication.
