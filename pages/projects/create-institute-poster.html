Create Institute · Stakeholder project

Make creativity easier to find.

A campus poster that helps students discover three Milwaukee School of Engineering creativity hubs: The COVE, the Innovent Center, and the ATC.

Strategy Visual design Accessibility

The brief

Turn three places into one clear invitation.

Working directly through the Milwaukee School of Engineering, my team designed a poster for stakeholders that was fun, engaging, and useful.

The piece gives students a simple resource for finding three separate creativity hubs on campus. The project was presented in a competition format, and the poster remained visible around campus years after the work was completed.
