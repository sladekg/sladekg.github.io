Discovery World · AI hackathon

Accessibility that meets people where they are.

A cost-effective museum companion using Bluetooth repeaters and an age-aware chatbot to help more visitors explore Discovery World.

Product design Research Accessibility

The challenge

A more welcoming way to explore.

In partnership with the Milwaukee School of Engineering Artificial Intelligence Club and Discovery World, our team was challenged to increase accessibility with a cost-effective, technology-enabled concept.

Our proposal used Bluetooth repeaters to locate visitors throughout the museum and a chatbot to share exhibit information. Responses were shaped by age so children, teens, and adults could all receive an appropriate experience.

The result connected wayfinding, learning, and personalization into one approachable museum companion.
