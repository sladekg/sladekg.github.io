The project

A new tool, built from the ground up.

At L & S Electric, Inc., I helped shape the LS-ACT excitation and governor system configuration tool from its earliest stages. I worked alongside a team of computer scientists and software engineers to bring the product to life.

I led user experience research and design as well as product design, grounding the work in customer needs and keeping the user experience connected to the product the team was building.

Research & direction

Understand the people behind the system.

I led customer research and used multiple methods to explore and evaluate the experience, including journey mapping, heuristic evaluations, and A/B testing. I also sketched early concepts to make possible directions tangible and support discussion.

These activities helped connect customer perspectives to product decisions throughout the design process.

Collaboration & delivery

Keep design and engineering moving together.

I was the direct point of contact for the engineering team, translating between user experience considerations and implementation as the product took shape. I also managed the product backlog to help organize and track the team's work.

To support people using the tool, I designed its user manual alongside the product experience.
