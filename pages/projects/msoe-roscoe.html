Milwaukee School of Engineering · Client project

Helping future students find their place.

A conversational AI prototype for MSOE Marketing that helps prospective students discover clubs, sports, classes, and campus life.

Product design Conversational AI Strategy

The brief

Make campus life easier to discover.

Working with the Milwaukee School of Engineering marketing department, our team created a new version of the university's Roscoe chatbot.

We focused on conversational AI practices that would help prospective students ask natural questions and find relevant opportunities across campus. The fully functional prototype was presented to marketing stakeholders for feedback.

This project sharpened my interest in the intersection of content strategy, product design, and helpful AI.
